## Memory: root cause of the −0.62 GB

Wisp's App, Wired and Compressed matched AM's to a few hundredths, but AM's own Memory Used is larger than its
App + Wired + Compressed by 0.60–0.69 GB. AM's Used is **Physical − Free − Cached Files**, not the sum of the
breakdown. Matching each AM refresh to `vm_stat` sampled every 100 ms (the sample whose App, Wired, Compressed and
Cached Files equal AM's, error ≤ 0.03 GB) showed:

- **Cached Files** = `external_page_count + purgeable_count`, within 0.005 GB.
- Physical − every page queue (free, active, inactive, speculative, throttled, wired, compressor) is a constant
  **0.603 GB**, which is `vm.page_booter_count` = 39 569 pages = 0.604 GB: memory outside every VM queue. AM counts it
  as used; App + Wired + Compressed cannot.
- What is left (0.04–0.10 GB, growing with pressure) is the free count. Over 36 matched refreshes, AM Used − formula:

| Free used in Physical − Free − Cached | mean | min | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| none: App + Wired + Compressed (Wisp before the fix) | +0.707 | +0.690 | +0.761 |
| `vm_stat` "Pages free" (`free_count − speculative_count`) | +0.103 | +0.086 | +0.158 |
| `free_count` | +0.131 | +0.088 | +0.213 |
| `vm.page_free_count` | +0.037 | +0.021 | +0.094 |
| **`vm.page_free_count − vm.page_free_cpu_count`** | **+0.007** | −0.021 | +0.067 |

The fix (`fad174a`, test first: the new test failed with Used = 20.48 GB against 29.12 GB expected) makes
`Memory::used()` = total − free − cached, with free from the last row and `vm_stat`'s free count as the fallback when
those sysctls are missing. The free pair is an empirical fit on this M5 under macOS 27; the 0.6 GB booter term is the
part that matters, and is exact. Spec §4.1 is updated to match (`18978fd`).
